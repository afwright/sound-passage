Not for navigation. Forecast and model output only. Wind, tide and current values come from public models (Open-Meteo, NOAA CO-OPS predictions, NWS) and the routes and ETA are a rough planning aid — they ignore traffic separation lanes, wakes, rips, ferries, fog and your boat's real performance, and the depth check uses a smoothed bathymetry grid that can miss rocks, pilings and narrow shoals. Check the NOAA charts, the current NWS forecast and Notices to Mariners before leaving the dock, and make your own call.
